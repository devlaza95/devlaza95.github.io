Jarvis

A personal assistant with one user: its author.

Jarvis runs on its author's own computer and talks to them through a private Telegram chat. It is not a service for other people, has no sign-up, and no one else's account is connected to it.

What it does with Google

With the author's permission, given on Google's consent screen, Jarvis reads the author's Gmail inbox and Google Calendar. Access is read-only. Jarvis uses it to:

  • summarize new mail and flag what needs attention, and
  • answer the author's questions, such as "what came in today?" or "what is on my calendar tomorrow?".

Jarvis cannot send, delete or change mail or calendar events.

Privacy

What Jarvis reads, where it goes and how to revoke it is in the privacy policy.
